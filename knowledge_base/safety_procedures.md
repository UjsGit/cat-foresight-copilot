# CAT ForeSight Copilot - Safety Procedures & Operating Guidelines

## Core Safety Principles
1. **Zero Compromise on Blind-Spot Clearances**: Heavy equipment cabs have extensive visual blind zones, particularly behind the counterweight and on the off-cab side.
2. **Pedestrian Exclusion Zones**: Maintain a minimum 7.0-meter dynamic buffer around active excavators, wheel loaders, and articulated trucks.
3. **Seatbelt Interlock Rule**: Always fasten the 3-point harness before releasing the hydraulic safety lock lever or disengaging the parking brake.

## High-Risk Operating Scenarios

### 1. Reversing in Reduced Visibility
- **Protocol**: Whenever rainfall exceeds 2.0 mm/hr or visibility drops below 400 meters, sound horn with two short blasts before initiating reverse gear.
- **Speed Limit**: Maximum 5.0 km/h in reverse on wet or unconsolidated terrain.
- **Copilot Action**: Real-time camera proximity alert will flash CRITICAL if any person enters within 7.0 meters. Immediate controlled deceleration and visual confirmation required.

### 2. Slope Operations (Inclines > 8°)
- Keep bucket low (30-50 cm above ground) while traversing.
- Never turn across a slope exceeding 10°; travel straight up or down.
- Monitor hydraulic oil temperature—steep grade operation increases hydraulic backpressure and thermal load.

### 3. Trenching & Underground Clearance
- Maintain at least 1.5x trench depth offset from the trench edge for excavator tracks.
- Check spotter signals prior to full swing rotations.
