# Efficient Idle Management & Fuel Conservation

## The Cost of Unnecessary Idling
- Modern heavy construction excavators consume between 2.5 and 4.2 litres of diesel per hour at low idle, and up to 8.0 litres/hour at high idle.
- Extended idling without work cycles also causes soot buildup in the Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF), requiring frequent forced regenerations.

## Recommended Operational Protocols
1. **The 3-Minute Rule**: If waiting for haul trucks exceeds 3 minutes, throttle down to auto-idle (800 RPM). If wait exceeds 6 minutes, engage engine stop.
2. **Terrain & Slope Impact on Fuel Consumption**:
   - Operating on a 12° slope increases fuel burn rate by approximately 15-20% due to continuous hydraulic pump displacement to maintain boom stability and slew swing brake resistance.
   - High payload weight (>15 tonnes) combined with steep grade can trigger explainable fuel consumption anomalies (+18% vs baseline) without operator inefficiency.
3. **Productivity vs Economy Modes**:
   - Use Power/Productivity Mode for hard rock fracturing or high-bank aggregate loading.
   - Switch to Economy Mode for general stockpiling, trench backfilling, and clean-up.
