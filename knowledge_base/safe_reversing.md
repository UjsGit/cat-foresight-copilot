# Safe Reversing & Blind Spot Hazard Management

## Reversing Hazards Overview
Excavators and large wheel loaders have rear blind zones extending up to 12 meters behind the counterweight. When ground personnel, survey engineers, or service trucks enter this zone during reversing maneuvers, reaction times are drastically reduced.

## Proximity Alert Hierarchy
- **CRITICAL (> 60 Risk Score / < 5.0m proximity)**:
  - Audible cabin alarm sounds.
  - Full-screen amber/red alert banner.
  - Immediate operator directive: **"STOP — PERSON IN REAR HAZARD ZONE"**.
  - Operator must bring machine to a complete stop, switch to neutral, and check camera/mirrors before acknowledging.
- **WARNING (30-59 Risk Score / 5.0m - 10.0m proximity)**:
  - Yellow visual banner.
  - Advised reduction of speed to < 3 km/h.
- **INFO (< 30 Risk Score)**:
  - Quiet perimeter visual radar marker on glanceable HUD.

## Wet Weather & Low Visibility Adjustments
- In rainfall > 5 mm/hr, braking distance on loose aggregate increases by 35-50%.
- The ForeSight Copilot expands the critical warning threshold from 5.0m to 7.5m automatically during rain.
