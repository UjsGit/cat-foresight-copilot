# Machine Warnings & Fault Code Resolution Guide

## Diagnostic Fault Codes

### E-304: Hydraulic Oil Over-Temperature
- **Trigger**: Hydraulic tank temperature sensor > 95°C for more than 30 consecutive seconds.
- **Cause**: Sustained high relief pressure, steep slope grade (>10°), heavy continuous cycle digging, or clogged oil cooler radiator fins.
- **Operator Action**:
  1. Reduce engine RPM to low idle (800-1000 RPM).
  2. Park on level ground and keep hydraulic lock engaged.
  3. Let engine idle for 3-5 minutes to circulate coolant through the oil cooler.
  4. Inspect radiator fins for debris accumulation.

### E-108: Engine Coolant High Temperature
- **Trigger**: Engine cylinder head / coolant temperature > 98°C.
- **Cause**: Heavy load operation, high ambient temp (>35°C), low coolant level.
- **Operator Action**:
  1. Disengage high productivity mode and switch to Economy mode.
  2. Idle machine; do NOT immediately shut down engine to avoid thermal shock.

### W-042: Fuel Filter Pressure Variance
- **Trigger**: Differential pressure across primary fuel filter exceeds 15 kPa.
- **Cause**: Contaminated diesel or wax accumulation in cold weather.
- **Operator Action**: Notify maintenance at shift conclusion; finish current loading cycle at reduced RPM.
