# Pre-Start Inspection Checklist Standard

## Daily Mandatory Walkaround Items

1. **Safety Restraint System**:
   - Seatbelt web integrity check.
   - Buckle latch sensor confirmation on cab console.
2. **Vision & Proximity Sensors**:
   - Camera lens cleanliness (front, rear, off-cab blind spot).
   - Radar and ultrasonic proximity sensor dome cleanliness.
3. **Fluids & Filters**:
   - Engine oil dipstick level within safe crosshatch.
   - Hydraulic oil level visible in upper sight glass.
   - Diesel fuel level above 25% minimum threshold for shift start.
4. **No Active Critical DTCs**:
   - ECM health score above 80.
   - Zero critical red fault codes active.
5. **Weather & Terrain Review**:
   - Review rainfall forecast, wind gusts, and ground stability conditions for assigned quarry/excavation zone.
6. **Task Assignment Confirmation**:
   - Verify task ID, target payload tonnes, dump truck cycle cadence, and boundary geofence.
