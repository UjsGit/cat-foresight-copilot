# Heavy Machinery & Telemetry Glossary

- **ECM (Engine Control Module)**: Electronic brain controlling fuel injection timing, turbo boost, and emission control sensors.
- **Hydraulic Pressure (bar / psi)**: Pressure inside the main pump circuit. Normal operation: 180-320 bar; relief valve limit: 350 bar.
- **Cycle Time (seconds)**: Time required to complete one complete dig-swing-dump-return sequence.
- **Payload (tonnes)**: Net material mass contained in the bucket or truck bed measured by onboard load cells.
- **Proximity Sensor**: Ultrasonic and radar sensor array scanning 360° perimeter around counterweight and undercarriage.
- **Terrain Slope (degrees)**: Ground pitch and roll inclination angle affecting center of gravity and engine load.
- **Adaptive ETA**: Machine learning predicted shift/task completion timestamp factoring in weather, slope, payload, and site traffic.
- **IsolationForest Anomaly Score**: Multivariate statistical anomaly index quantifying deviation from nominal operating baselines.
